## 💔 SLIDE 2: The Ground Reality of Bharat
* **₹4,00,000+ Crores:** Annual welfare outlay across Central and State Governments (PM-Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, PMAY, PM SVANidhi, PM Vishwakarma, etc.).
* **The Irony:** Over **70% of eligible rural & urban poor** miss out on life-changing benefits.
* **The 3 Bottlenecks:**
  1. *Information Asymmetry:* 800+ schemes with fragmented portals and complex eligibility criteria.
  2. *The Single-Document Trap:* Missing one document (e.g. Domicile or Khatauni) results in outright application rejection.
  3. *Zero Accountability:* When DBT transfers or pensions are delayed for months, citizens have no institutional recourse.

## 🧠 SLIDE 4: Multi-Agent Swarm Architecture
Jan-Sahayak breaks down the complex civic delivery challenge into 5 specialized autonomous agents:
1. **Profile & Doc Intelligence Agent:** Ingests vernacular voice, text, or KYC and normalizes into a 12-factor citizen schema.
2. **Bharat Schemes Reasoning Engine:** Audits demographic rules against Central & State schemes with zero hallucinations.
3. **Compliance & Gap Verifier Agent:** Dissects in-hand documents vs scheme mandates and generates an actionable remediation plan.
4. **Autonomous Form & PDF Packager:** Compiles verified profile into an official, publication-grade application dossier PDF.
5. **CPGRAMS Grievance Redressal Agent:** Formulates legally binding administrative appeals citing Public Services Guarantee Acts.
